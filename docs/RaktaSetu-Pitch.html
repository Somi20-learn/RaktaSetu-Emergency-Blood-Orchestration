Ignition Hackathon MVP — Node.js · Express Team of 4
1

The Problem

In a trauma emergency, the golden hour is spent on the phone. Hospitals call blood bank after blood bank, and chase donors separately, with no live view of who actually has the right group in stock nearby.

  • Blood availability is fragmented across banks, hospitals, and donors — there is no single real-time view.
  • Matching is done manually and only by exact group — compatibility and expiry are ignored under pressure.
  • Meanwhile, lakhs of units are wasted in India every year — much of it simply expiring unused on a shelf a few kilometres away.
2

The Solution

RaktaSetu connects hospitals, blood banks, and eligible donors on one platform and turns "find blood" into an orchestrated, prioritized workflow.

1 · RequestHospital raises a request: group, units, urgency.
2 · MatchEngine ranks sources by compatibility, stock, distance, urgency & expiry.
3 · ReserveReserve units at the best source in one tap.
4 · EscalateNo fit? Auto-escalate to the next-best source instantly.
3

Blood-bank finders already exist (e-RaktKosh, Friends2Support). RaktaSetu wins by doing what a finder can't: preventing shortages and eliminating waste across the whole network.

Key Differentiators

1

Predictive Shortage Engine

Forecasts which group will run short, at which hospital, in the next few days — from usage patterns and seasonal spikes (accidents, dengue, festivals).

Proactive, not reactive
2

Zero-Waste Redistribution

Detects near-expiry units in surplus banks and routes them to high-demand banks before they expire (First-Expiry-First-Out).

Turns waste into lives
3

Multi-Bank Orchestration

When no single bank can supply 6 units, it splits and routes the order across 2–3 banks — logistics, not a phone book.

On-brand: orchestration
4

Donor SOS for Rare Groups

For O− and the Bombay group, auto-broadcasts an SOS to nearby eligible donors — respecting the 90-day donation gap.

Reaches where banks can't
5

Component-Level Intelligence

Matches actual components hospitals need — PRBC, platelets, plasma, cryo — not a generic "blood" bucket.

Real clinical depth
6

Live Green-Corridor Map

Animates blood moving bank → hospital with live ETA on a map; a path to drone delivery for critical cases.

Demo-day wow factor
"e-RaktKosh and others help you find blood. RaktaSetu orchestrates the entire network — predicting shortages before they happen and redistributing blood before it expires — so no patient waits and no unit is wasted."
